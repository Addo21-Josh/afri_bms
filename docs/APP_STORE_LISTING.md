### Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
```
Run your retail store, supermarket, pharmacy, or wholesale business with Afri_BMS — the modern Point of Sale (POS) and inventory management system built specifically for businesses across Africa and emerging markets.

Whether you're operating online or in areas with intermittent internet connectivity, Afri_BMS works 100% offline and automatically syncs with the cloud whenever you reconnect.

KEY FEATURES:

🛒 RAPID POS CHECKOUT REGISTER
• Fast barcode scanning using your phone's camera or wireless barcode readers.
• Instant product lookup by name, category, or SKU.
• Multi-payment support: Cash, Mobile Money (MoMo), Bank Card, Split Payments, and Store Credit.
• Instant discount application and customizable tax handling.

📦 INTELLIGENT INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
• Track stock levels in real-time across multiple product categories.
• Automated low-stock alerts and smart reorder notifications so you never run out of bestsellers.
• Stock movements ledger: track damages, returns, adjustments, and supplier restocks.
• In-app barcode generator: create EAN-13, Code-128, and QR codes for all products.

🧾 RECEIPTS & PRINTING
• Print directly to portable 58mm & 80mm Bluetooth and WiFi thermal receipt printers.
• Generate sleek PDF receipts and share instantly via WhatsApp or email.
• Send automated SMS receipts directly to customer mobile numbers.

👥 CUSTOMER & SUPPLIER LEDGER (DEBT TRACKING)
• Track customer credit balances and overdue debtor accounts.
• Record partial debt payments and view complete payment histories.
• Supplier directory to manage purchase orders, pending deliveries, and balances.

🏢 MULTI-BRANCH & STAFF MANAGEMENT
• Manage multiple store branches from a single unified dashboard.
• Staff accounts with role-based permissions (Cashier, Store Manager, Owner).
• Track cashier shifts, sales per staff member, and prevent unauthorized price alterations.

📊 PROFIT & LOSS FINANCIAL REPORTS
• Real-time financial hub: daily gross revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), net profit, and margins.
• Cash flow breakdown and overhead expense tracking.
• Export financial data and inventory valuation sheets to CSV / Excel.

⚡ OFFLINE-FIRST & SECURE
• Works uninterrupted without internet connection.
• Bank-grade encrypted storage for tokens and credentials.
• Cloud synchronization powered by Supabase with multi-tenant row-level security.

PRICING PLANS:
• Free Starter: Perfect for solo shops (Up to 50 products, 1 branch, standard POS).
• Afri_BMS Pro (GH₵ 50/mo): Up to 500 products, 5 branches, thermal printing, and auto-sync.
• Afri_BMS Enterprise (GH₵ 150/mo): Unlimited products, unlimited branches & staff, custom roles.

Transform your business operations today with Afri_BMS — Smarter Business, Bigger Profits!

Support: support@afribms.com
Website: https://afribms.com
```
